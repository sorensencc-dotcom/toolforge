[MEMOIR EXCERPT: CHARLES E. SORENSEN - MY FORTY YEARS WITH FORD]
Chapter 18: Willow Run Production Layout and Mass Production Transfer.

In late 1940, officials from the War Department requested that Ford Motor Company analyze B-24 bomber manufacturing processes. Major General Arnold urged Ford to build subassemblies for Consolidated Aircraft. However, preliminary evaluations indicated that aircraft manufacturing methods relied on manual hand-fitting rather than interchangeable tooling.

To address this challenge, Sorensen traveled to California in January 1941 to inspect the plant.

.........................................................At the Hotel del Coronado in January 1941, I spent the night drafting a pencil layout applying cast-iron automotive tooling and continuous progressive assembly line principles to the Consolidated B-24 airframe.

This sketch formed the master blueprint for Willow Run.
