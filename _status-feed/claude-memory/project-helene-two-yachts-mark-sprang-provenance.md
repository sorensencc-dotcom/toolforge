Charles E. Sorensen owned two separate yachts both named Helene, easily conflated:
- **Helene I** — Defoe Boat & Motor Works, Bay City MI, built 1927, US Official No. 226316, homeport Detroit (Detroit Yacht Club burgee). Owned 1927–c.1936, then several more owners into the 1980s.
- **Helene II** — Bath Iron Works, Bath ME, delivered 1931, US Registry 230716. The larger ocean-going yacht; became USS/USAV Cavanaugh in WWII, sank 1989 off Cardiff, Wales.

TRM topics: `charlie/helene` (Helene II, from `helene.txt`/`Helene History.doc`) and `charlie/helene-i` (Helene I, from the 44-image `US_226316_*.jpg` batch in `C:\dev\research-data\raw\helene`), crosslinked to each other and to `charlie/benson-ford`.

**Provenance (confirmed by user 2026-08-01):** the Helene I documents/images came from Mark Sprang, Archivist, Historical Collections of the Great Lakes, Jerome Library, Bowling Green State University, Bowling Green OH 43403 (419-372-9613).
