# B-17 Production Claim Debunking & B-24 Engineering Reality (GAP-03)

> Auto-healed research dossier resolving the historical discrepancy regarding heavy bomber manufacturing at the Ford Willow Run plant.

## The Contradiction & Claim Analysis

### Erroneous Video Claim
A transcript asserted that Boeing's **B-17 Flying Fortress** was manufactured as the primary heavy bomber at the Ford Willow Run facility in Ypsilanti, Michigan.

### Archival Refutation & Historical Reality
Primary War Production Board (WPB) contracts, Charles E. Sorensen’s manufacturing records, and Ford Aircraft Building Division blueprints prove that **Willow Run was engineered, tooled, and constructed exclusively for the Consolidated B-24 Liberator**:

1. **Exclusivity of B-24 Liberator Production**:
   * Ford produced **8,685 complete and knock-down B-24 Liberators** (models B-24E, B-24H, B-24J, B-24L, and B-24M) at Willow Run between 1942 and June 28, 1945.
   * Zero Boeing B-17 airframes were manufactured or assembled at Willow Run.
2. **The "B.V.D." Pool (Actual B-17 Manufacturers)**:
   * B-17 production was executed under a tripartite joint manufacturing pool consisting of:
     * **B**: Boeing Aircraft Company (Seattle and Renton, WA).
     * **V**: Vega Aircraft Corporation / Lockheed (Burbank, CA).
     * **D**: Douglas Aircraft Company (Long Beach, CA).
3. **Dedicated Tooling Architecture**:
   * Albert Kahn's factory layout and Charles E. Sorensen's stamping dies (cast in iron and kirksite) were engineered specifically around the Davis high-aspect-ratio wing design and Fowler flaps unique to the Consolidated B-24.
