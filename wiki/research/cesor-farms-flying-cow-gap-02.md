# The Flying Cow of Willow Run: Cesor Maxim's Irene (GAP-02)

> Primary archival dossier resolving the historical record for the November 1947 air cargo transport of Charles E. Sorensen's prize Guernsey cow, *Cesor Maxim's Irene*, from Willow Run Airport.

## Historical & Biographical Context

Following Charles E. Sorensen’s resignation from Ford Motor Company in 1944 and his tenure as president of Willys-Overland:
* Sorensen owned and operated **Cesor Farms** (near New Hudson and Farmington, MI; managed by Albert Kessler), an agricultural venture breeding championship Guernsey cattle.
* In **November 1947**, *Cesor Maxim's Irene* was sold for **$3,100** to Ivan W. Byers of Shoal Falls Farm (Hendersonville, North Carolina).
* To prevent transit fatigue and "travel sickness" associated with multi-day rail transport, the cow was booked on a commercial air cargo flight originating from Willow Run Airport.
* This shipment marked the **first bovine passenger ever loaded and transported by air from Willow Run Airport**.
