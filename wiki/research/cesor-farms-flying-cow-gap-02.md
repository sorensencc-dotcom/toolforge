# The Flying Cow of Willow Run: Cesor Maxim's Irene (GAP-02)

> Stage 3 deep research directive for `trm-worker`. This dossier tracks the historical and archival evidence surrounding the 1947 air cargo flight of Charles E. Sorensen's prize Guernsey cow, *Cesor Maxim's Irene*, from Willow Run Airport.

## Historical & Biographical Context

Following Charles E. Sorensen’s resignation from Ford Motor Company in 1944 and his subsequent role as president of Willys-Overland:
* Sorensen operated **Cesor Farms** (Farmington, MI), a high-profile agricultural venture specializing in purebred Guernsey cattle breeding stock and mechanized farming experimentation.
* In **1947**, following the handover of the Willow Run manufacturing complex and airfield to **Kaiser-Frazer** and the transition of the airfield into Detroit’s primary commercial passenger and air freight hub, *Cesor Maxim's Irene* was loaded onto an air transport for a publicized cargo flight.
* This event positioned Sorensen's personal agricultural enterprise at the vanguard of early commercial air freight logistics.

## Target Archival Collections

### 1. Detroit Regional & Agricultural News Archives (1946–1948)
* *The Detroit Free Press* and *The Detroit News* contemporary feature articles, photographs, and freight manifests.
* *The Michigan Farmer* and American Guernsey Cattle Club (*AGCC*) journals documenting Cesor Farms show herds and livestock shipments.

### 2. Willow Run Airport Operations & Air Freight Logs
* Early commercial cargo manifests for non-scheduled and scheduled freight carriers operating out of Willow Run (e.g., Slick Airways, Flying Tiger Line, Capital Airlines).
