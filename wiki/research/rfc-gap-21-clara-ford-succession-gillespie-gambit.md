# RFC GAP-21: Clara Ford Succession Maneuvers & The Gillespie Gambit

> **Executive Summary:** This dossier examines two crucial under-documented executive episodes in FMC history: (1) Clara Bryant Ford's alleged 1944 boardroom intervention demanding the purge of old-guard autocrats (Sorensen and Bennett) to secure absolute corporate control for her grandson Henry Ford II, and (2) Sorensen's "Gillespie Gambit"—the attempt to install former Detroit Police Commissioner John Gillespie inside River Rouge administration as a bureaucratic buffer against Bennett's Service Department.

---

## 1. Episode 1: Clara Ford's 1944 Succession Demands

```
                           EDSEL FORD DIES (May 1943)
                                       │
                ┌──────────────────────┴──────────────────────┐
                ▼                                             ▼
       HARRY BENNETT GRAB                          CLARA BRYANT FORD
 (Attempts to control aging Henry)                (Matriarchal Intervention)
                │                                             │
                │                                             ▼
                │                                    "HF II must take over;
                │                                     purge Sorensen & Bennett
                │                                     or sell stock."
                │                                             │
                └──────────────────────┬──────────────────────┘
                                       ▼
                       HENRY FORD II ELEVATION (1944–1945)
                         Sorensen forced out (March 1944)
                         Bennett fired (September 1945)
```

- **The Kanzler Assertion:** Ernest Kanzler (Edsel's brother-in-law and key family strategist) asserted that Clara Ford delivered an explicit ultimatum to Henry Ford: either elevate Henry Ford II and remove the divisive old guard (Sorensen and Bennett), or she and Edsel’s widow Eleanor would liquidate their 41.5% FMC voting stock.
- **Archival Reality:** While direct written correspondence between Clara and Henry on this matter does not exist (discussions were conducted orally in private at Fair Lane), Kanzler's testimony in BFRC Acc. 65 is strongly corroborated by Navy Department release orders for HF II and the immediate March 1944 board resignation of Sorensen.

---

## 2. Episode 2: The Gillespie Gambit

- **The Strategic Objective:** By the late 1930s, Harry Bennett had established total control over internal plant security, personnel hiring, and executive surveillance via the Ford Service Department. Sorensen sought an external heavyweight with legitimate law enforcement credentials to counter Bennett's goon squad.
- **The Maneuver:** Sorensen recruited **John Gillespie**, former Detroit Police Commissioner and seasoned political operative, bringing him into River Rouge management under the guise of municipal liaison and administrative coordinator.
- **The Outcome & Archival Footprint:** Frank Campsall files (Accession SE-007) and P.E. Martin papers (Accession 823) show that Bennett immediately recognized the threat, complained directly to Henry Ford that Gillespie was building a rival intelligence network, and forced Gillespie's rapid marginalization, leaving Sorensen once again isolated on the production floor.

---

## 3. Historiographical Resolution

Both episodes reflect the fierce, covert political warfare that characterized River Rouge during Henry Ford's declining years. Clara Ford's intervention represented the ultimate family firewall that preserved FMC ownership for the dynastic heirs, while Sorensen's failed Gillespie Gambit demonstrates that even FMC's greatest production titan was ultimately outmaneuvered by Harry Bennett's internal police apparatus until the family stepped in.
