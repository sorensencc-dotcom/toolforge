# CIC - Ford Executive Dynamics: Unresolved Contradictions Reconciliation

> Reconciles the five core historical contradictions in the Cast Iron Charlie project dossier (`0caf6707-f8f2-4d2a-acd2-020acead55ba`) cross-referencing corporate memoirs and primary archival accessions.

## Reconciliation Matrix

| # | Topic | Grounded Resolution | Evidence Tier | Treatment & Dossier Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Moving Assembly Line Origins** | Henry Ford acted as executive sponsor rather than technical inventor. Charles Sorensen, P.E. Martin, Clarence Avery, and C. Harold Wills engineered shop-floor flow from 1908 Piquette Model N experiments through 1913 Highland Park power line. Grounded in Sorensen 1956 memoir (Ch. 10) and BFRC Acc. 499 (Klann). | **Tier 1/2** (VERIFIED) | Frame assembly line as iterative collective engineering sponsored by Ford, rather than sole executive flash of genius. |
| 2 | **Charles Sorensen: Protector vs. Frustrator** | Sorensen operated as Edsel's practical buffer against Bennett's Service Department (e.g., Oct 1942 kidnapping defense), but was an autonomous autocratic baron whose tenure frustrated family succession. Clara and Eleanor Ford opposed both men post-May 1943. | **Tier 2** (INFERRED dual role) | Depict Sorensen's complex dual loyalty: fierce institutional defender of the company/heirs, yet domineering power center whose presence stifled Edsel. |
| 3 | **Willow Run Layout vs. Production Crisis** | Sorensen's January 1941 Coronado Hotel pencil layout initialed by Edsel is verified. However, initial production suffered catastrophic tooling and scrap bottlenecks until late-1943 reorganization under Mead Bricker. Lindbergh May 1942 inspection confirmed. Specific granular "Edsel quality rescues" remain under-documented. | **Tier 1 Primary (Layout) / Tier 2 (Quality Bottlenecks)** | Maintain Coronado Hotel origin; portray realistic early manufacturing chaos ("Willit Run") and Lindbergh inspection. |
| 4 | **Henry Ford Dual Nature** | Sorensen quote authentic in 1956 memoir (Ch. 2): *"small-minded, suspicious, jealous, occasionally malicious, and lacking in sincerity"*, offset by genuine respect for Ford's visionary industrial scale. Do not elevate Clara 1944 ultimatum beyond secondary status without primary notes. |
