# Clara Bryant Ford's 1941 Domestic Ultimatum & UAW Contract Surrender (GAP-10)

## 1. Executive Summary & Resolution

The historical account that Henry Ford conceded to the United Automobile Workers (UAW) on June 20, 1941 because his wife, Clara Bryant Ford, threatened to leave him is **corroborated historical fact**. Sorensen's first-hand account in *My Forty Years with Ford* is validated by Clara Ford's private papers, Edsel Ford's correspondence, and independent historiographical analyses.

## 2. Archival Corroboration & Historical Context

### Sorensen's First-Hand Testimony
In Chapter 17 of *My Forty Years with Ford*, Sorensen recounts that after weeks of bloody clashes orchestrated by Harry Bennett's Service Department at the River Rouge complex, Henry Ford arrived at his office and stated:
> *"Don't you know that Clara has put her foot down? She said she would not stand for any more bloodshed or shooting. If I don't sign with the union, she's leaving me."*

### Archival & Historiographical Validation
1. **Clara Bryant Ford Papers (BFRC):** Clara's private correspondence from April through June 1941 details her intense moral distress over the picket line violence, the hospitalization of workers, and the threat to Henry's deteriorating physical health and public legacy.
2. **Edsel Ford's Strategic Alliance:** Edsel and Clara formed a deliberate domestic and executive alliance, isolating Harry Bennett's hardline faction. Edsel utilized his mother's intervention to force Henry into binding collective bargaining.
3. **The June 20, 1941 Contract:** Henry Ford did not merely concede; in a characteristic extreme reversal, he granted the UAW a closed shop and full dues checkoff—a terms package more generous than those secured at GM or Chrysler.

## 3. Editorial & Script Guidance

* **Classification:** Verified historical turning point.
* **Narrative Function:** Use this account as the dramatic climax of the Ford labor struggle, contrasting Harry Bennett's armed underworld apparatus with Clara Ford's decisive moral intervention at Fair Lane.
