# Prioritized Archival Search Plan — Ferguson Depositions & Co-Executive Papers

> Grounded archival search protocol targeting Harry Ferguson v. Ford Motor Co. litigation records and co-executive papers to corroborate Charles E. Sorensen's (CESOR) single-sourced claims regarding tractor development, the 1938 handshake agreement, and patent validity.

## 1. Verified Repository Target: Benson Ford Research Center (BFRC)

The strongest verified repository for the Ferguson litigation records is the **Ford Motor Company Legal records collection, Office of the General Counsel records series** held at the Benson Ford Research Center (The Henry Ford, Dearborn, MI):

|---|---|---|---|---|

| **Acc. 380** | 1914–1947 | 12.4 cu. ft. + 3 oversize | Henry Ford office files: correspondence, financial reports, advertising samples, agricultural trade journals, and tractor development files. | **VERIFIED** ([ArchiveGrid](https://researchworks.oclc.org/archivegrid/data/56572242)). |
| **Acc. 1753** | — | Unprocessed | "Ferguson v. Ford Lawsuit Records" listed under New & Unprocessed accessions. | **VERIFIED** to exist; detailed inventory pending. |

## 2. Evidentiary Case Facts & Date Corrections

1. **Complaint Filing Date**: *Ferguson et al. v. Ford Motor Co. et al.* (S.D.N.Y.) was filed on **January 8, 1948** (8 F.R.D. 414; 92 F. Supp. 868). (Note: January 9, 1948 was the initial press coverage date in the New York Times; use January 8 in official repository inquiries).
2. **Scale of Sworn Discovery**: Over 25,000 pages of testimony and depositions were generated by July 15, 1949 (89 F. Supp. 45). Overseas depositions took place in London, Leamington Spa, and Belfast in summer 1950.
3. **Settlement**: Consent judgment entered in April 1952 for \$9.25M against an initial claim of \$251M (later amended to \$341M).
4. **Docket Citation Safeguard**: The historical docket citation "44,482" remains **unverified** in primary court records. Inquiries must cite the published case citations (8 F.R.D. 414, 89 F. Supp. 45, 92 F. Supp. 868) and case title rather than the unverified docket number.

## 3. Inquiry Priorities & Next Steps

1. **Priority 1 (BFRC Acc. 375 & 1753)**: Submit formal research query to Benson Ford Research Center asking whether complete deposition transcripts for Charles E. Sorensen, Henry Ford II, or Harry Ferguson are preserved, or if only bound excerpts survived.
2. **Priority 2 (NARA Kansas City vs NYC RG 21)**: Hold National Archives RG 21 civil case-file requests until BFRC scope is determined and the S.D.N.Y. transfer inventory to Kansas City is confirmed.
