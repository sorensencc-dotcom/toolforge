# Sorensen Monday Keelhauling Meetings (GAP-03)

> Stage 3 deep research directive for `trm-worker`. This file tracks the primary-source investigation into Charles E. Sorensen's weekly production meetings and superintendents conferences at the Ford Rouge plant.

## Research Objective

Resolve whether the legendary *"Monday Keelhauling"* sessions were:
1. Formally scheduled manufacturing committee conferences with preserved stenographic minutes.
2. Informal executive enforcement sessions recounted primarily through oral history and post-Ford memoirs.
3. Attended or counter-balanced by Harry Bennett's Service Department and Edsel Ford's executive staff.

## Primary Archival Target Sets

### 1. Bentley Historical Library (University of Michigan)
* **Charles E. Sorensen Papers (1904–1965)** (`Call No: 85150 Aa 2`):
  * Box 1: Executive Correspondence.
  * Box 2: Production Memoranda (1935–1944).
* **Henry Ford II Corporate Reorganization Files** (`Call No: 89376 Aa 2`):
  * Executive Committee transcripts during the 1943–1945 transition.

### 2. Benson Ford Research Center (The Henry Ford)
* **Ford Motor Company Production Meeting Minutes** (`Accession 65 / Accession 390`):
  * Rouge plant superintendents meeting records and weekly output reviews.
