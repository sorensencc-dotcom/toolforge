# B-24 Knock-Down Kit Manufacturing & Overland Logistics (GAP-06)

> Technical dossier detailing the sub-assembly manufacturing and overland transit logistics of Consolidated B-24 knock-down kits from Ford's Willow Run facility.

## Industrial Concept & Logistics Framework

When the War Production Board structured the Liberator Production Pool, Willow Run was tasked not only with assembling complete flyable aircraft, but also acting as the central master sub-assembly manufacturer for other assembly plants:
1. **Knock-Down (KD) Kit Concept**:
   * Complete sets of major sub-assemblies (outer wing panels, center wing sections, forward fuselage nose sections, empennage, and engine nacelles) were manufactured at Willow Run using automotive-style hard stamping dies.
   * Total knock-down kits produced at Willow Run: **1,893 units**.
2. **Destination Assembly Plants**:
   * **Consolidated Aircraft (Fort Worth, Texas)**: Assembled B-24s from Willow Run KD shipments.
   * **Douglas Aircraft Company (Tulsa, Oklahoma)**: Received complete knock-down kits for final assembly.
3. **Custom Overland Highway Logistics**:
   * Railroad boxcars and standard flatcars could not accommodate the fully assembled 55-foot center-wing section with engines and landing gear wells.
   * Ford engineered a fleet of **custom 60-foot articulated truck-trailer rigs** operated by Ford drivers.
   * Each specialized trailer carried two complete fuselage halves or one complete center-wing section with specialized rubber-cushioned jig mountings to eliminate structural deflection over highway transit.
