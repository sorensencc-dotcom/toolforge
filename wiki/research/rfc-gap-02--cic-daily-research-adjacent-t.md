# The Al-Toy "Jeep Day" Banquet (1945) (GAP-02)

> Research synthesis regarding post-war toy lore, industrial promotional campaigns, and the marketing launch of the civilian Jeep (CJ-2A) under Charles E. Sorensen and Willys-Overland.

## Historical & Industrial Background

In **July 1945**, Willys-Overland staged the historic **"Jeep Day"** at Charles E. Sorensen’s personal estate in Ohio and the Toledo plant, unveiling the **Willys Civilian Jeep (CJ-2A)** to the international automotive press:
* **The Al-Toy Partnership**: Toledo model maker **Al-Toy (Aluminum Toy Company)** was commissioned by Willys-Overland to produce high-precision, 1:16-scale cast aluminum models of the CJ-2A.
* **The Banquet Souvenirs**: These heavy, sand-cast aluminum toy jeeps were presented as executive banquet favors to attending journalists, distributors, and War Production Board officials.
* **Industrial Significance**: Marked the transition of military assembly capacity to civilian utility vehicles, utilizing promotional die-cast / sand-cast merchandise as dealer display models and promotional tools.
